# SEO and AI Search Visibility
**Evidence type:** Independent strategy project.

## Problem
Organic visibility can be fragmented across technical SEO, generic content, weak expertise signals and changing search journeys that include AI-generated answers.

## Proposed solution
- Map commercial intent and customer questions across awareness, consideration and decision.
- Build topic clusters around products, use cases, comparisons, pricing and support.
- Audit indexability, crawlability, page speed, internal links, schema eligibility and duplicate pages.
- Create original, expert-reviewed content with clear authorship, evidence and useful visuals.
- Improve product/service pages with proof, FAQs, comparisons, trust signals and clear CTAs.
- Track qualified organic leads/orders and assisted conversions, not rankings alone.

## KPIs
Non-brand clicks, qualified organic sessions, target-query visibility, indexed valuable pages, organic conversion rate, assisted revenue, engaged visits and lead quality.

## 60-day roadmap
Days 1–15: baseline and technical audit. Days 16–30: prioritise fixes and refresh high-intent pages. Days 31–45: publish cluster content. Days 46–60: evaluate conversions and refine.

## Guardrail
Search visibility changes are not guaranteed. Report actual Search Console and analytics data with date ranges.
