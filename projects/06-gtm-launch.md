# New Product Go-to-Market Plan
**Evidence type:** Independent strategy project.

## Problem
A product launch can create awareness but fail commercially if customer need, differentiation, price, distribution and sales readiness are not validated.

## GTM framework
1. Customer discovery: interviews, review mining and unmet-need analysis.
2. Segmentation: identify highest-potential customer groups and buying triggers.
3. Positioning: define target, category, differentiated benefit and proof.
4. Pricing: compare alternatives, willingness to pay, margin and channel economics.
5. Channel: select direct, marketplace, retail, distributor or partner routes.
6. Launch: pilot geography/audience, sales enablement, content and demand generation.
7. Scale gate: expand only after conversion, margin, supply and retention thresholds are met.

## Launch dashboard
Qualified demand, trial/sample-to-purchase conversion, distribution coverage, sell-through, CAC, gross margin, repeat rate, returns and customer feedback.

## Deliverables
Positioning statement, competitor map, launch calendar, budget, sales kit, pilot scorecard and post-launch review.
