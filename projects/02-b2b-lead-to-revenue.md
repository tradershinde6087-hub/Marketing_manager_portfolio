# B2B Lead-to-Revenue System
**Evidence type:** Independent strategy project; proposed operating model, not a claim of implementation.

## Problem
B2B businesses often generate enquiries but lose revenue through weak qualification, slow follow-up, unclear ownership and poor visibility between marketing and sales.

## Proposed solution
1. Define an ideal customer profile (industry, size, geography, need, buying role and fit).
2. Score leads by fit, intent and readiness; define MQL and SQL criteria with Sales.
3. Set a lead-response SLA and escalation process.
4. Standardise pipeline stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost.
5. Capture lead source, campaign, owner, next action, expected value and loss reason in CRM.
6. Run weekly pipeline reviews and monthly source-quality reviews.

## KPI scorecard
- Lead-to-contact rate and median response time
- MQL-to-SQL and SQL-to-opportunity conversion
- Meeting show rate and proposal win rate
- Sales cycle length and average deal size
- Pipeline coverage and sourced/influenced revenue
- Lost reasons and follow-up compliance

## 30-day pilot
Week 1: audit current funnel and agree definitions. Week 2: configure fields, stages and SLA. Week 3: train team and run pilot on one segment. Week 4: review conversion, data completeness and bottlenecks.

## Outcome definition
Success is measured against a documented baseline: faster response, improved qualified-opportunity rate, higher stage conversion and more reliable pipeline reporting. No uplift is claimed until pilot data exists.
