# Marketing Budget and Performance Dashboard
**Evidence type:** Illustrative management framework; replace sample values with authorised company data.

## Problem
Marketing reports often show activity but do not connect spend to qualified demand, pipeline, revenue or margin.

## Monthly dashboard sections
1. Budget: planned, actual, committed, variance and forecast.
2. Funnel: reach → visits → leads → qualified leads → opportunities → customers.
3. Economics: CPL, cost per qualified lead, CAC, AOV, gross margin and ROAS.
4. Revenue: sourced, influenced and repeat revenue with attribution caveats.
5. Channel: spend, volume, quality, conversion and next action.
6. Risks: tracking gaps, capacity constraints, stock, sales follow-up and data quality.

## Management decisions
Scale, maintain, test or stop each activity based on marginal economics, lead quality, strategic value and confidence in measurement.
