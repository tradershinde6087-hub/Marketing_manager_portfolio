# Paid Media ROI Recovery
**Evidence type:** Illustrative simulation framework; no real campaign results are claimed.

## Problem
Spend rises while lead quality, sales conversion or revenue attribution remains unclear. Platform-reported conversions may not equal incremental business outcomes.

## Diagnostic
- Audit pixels/tags, consent, UTMs, event definitions, CRM source capture and duplicate leads.
- Reconcile platform conversions with CRM-qualified leads, orders and finance data.
- Segment performance by audience, creative, placement, geography, device and landing page.
- Separate prospecting, retargeting and existing-customer campaigns.

## Optimisation plan
1. Pause only after checking sample size and conversion lag.
2. Test one variable at a time: offer, audience, creative or landing page.
3. Optimise to qualified leads or contribution where volume permits—not cheap clicks alone.
4. Add holdout or geo tests when feasible to estimate incrementality.
5. Reallocate budget using marginal cost per qualified outcome.

## KPI formulas
- CTR = clicks ÷ impressions
- CPC = spend ÷ clicks
- CPL = spend ÷ leads
- Cost per qualified lead = spend ÷ qualified leads
- CAC = acquisition spend ÷ new customers
- ROAS = attributed revenue ÷ ad spend
- Contribution after ads = revenue × contribution margin − ad spend

## Deliverables
Tracking audit, campaign taxonomy, test backlog, weekly scorecard and executive recommendation. Simulated data must be visibly marked as sample data.
