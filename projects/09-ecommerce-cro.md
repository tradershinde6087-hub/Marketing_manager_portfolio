# E-commerce Conversion Optimisation
**Evidence type:** Independent strategy project.

## Problem
Traffic does not translate into orders due to unclear product value, weak trust, poor mobile experience, hidden delivery costs or checkout friction.

## Audit
- Landing page message match and traffic intent
- Product imagery, descriptions, benefits and specifications
- Reviews, returns, delivery and payment clarity
- Mobile speed, navigation and search
- Cart and checkout errors
- Stock availability and pricing transparency

## Experiment backlog
Prioritise by expected impact, confidence, effort and risk. Test one major change at a time and define primary metric before launch.

## KPIs
Product-page conversion, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, cart abandonment, AOV, revenue per session, refund rate and contribution margin.

## Outcome reporting
Show baseline, sample size, test dates, variant, conversion difference and uncertainty. Do not claim causality from a simple before/after comparison.
