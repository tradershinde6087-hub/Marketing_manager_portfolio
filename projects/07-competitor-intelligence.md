# Competitor and Market Intelligence
**Evidence type:** Independent research template; populate with dated, cited market evidence before presenting conclusions.

## Problem
Teams make pricing and positioning decisions using anecdotal competitor observations or outdated information.

## Research dimensions
- Product range, features, pack sizes and claims
- Price per unit and promotional mechanics
- Distribution and marketplace availability
- Website and content positioning
- Review themes, complaints and unmet needs
- Target segments and channel partnerships
- Share of search / visibility indicators where data is available

## Output
A competitor matrix, perceptual map, opportunity gaps, risk assessment and recommended actions.

## Governance
Record source, URL, access date, geography and confidence for each finding. Do not present estimates as audited market share.
