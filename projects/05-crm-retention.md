# CRM and Customer Retention
**Evidence type:** Illustrative strategy project.

## Problem
Businesses over-invest in new acquisition while failing to identify customers due for reorder, at risk of churn or likely to respond to relevant cross-sell.

## Segmentation
- New customers: onboarding and education
- Active customers: usage support and relevant cross-sell
- Reorder due: replenishment reminder based on observed purchase cycle
- At-risk: no purchase beyond expected interval
- Lapsed: win-back based on value and reason for inactivity
- High-value: service, loyalty and feedback programme

## Journey design
Trigger → audience eligibility → message/offer → channel → conversion event → suppression rules → measurement.

## KPIs
Repeat purchase rate, time to second purchase, cohort retention, churn, reactivation rate, customer lifetime value, unsubscribe/complaint rate and incremental margin.

## Experiment
Randomly hold out a comparable eligible group from a campaign. Compare incremental orders and contribution, not only opens or clicks. Respect consent and communication preferences.
