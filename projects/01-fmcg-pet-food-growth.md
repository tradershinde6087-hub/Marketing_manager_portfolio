# FMCG / Pet Food Growth Case Study
**Evidence type:** Experience-based; figures are taken from the current resume and must be supported by employer-approved records before publication.

## Business context
Pet-food products serving pet owners, retailers, distributors and pet-care partners through B2B, B2C and D2C routes.

## Business challenge
Build product awareness and distribution, win new buyers, strengthen repeat ordering and compete on value in a category where trust, availability, price and product quality influence purchase.

## Workstreams
- **Market and competitor research:** map competing products, pack sizes, claims, pricing and channel availability.
- **Positioning and pricing:** communicate product value and price architecture; resume states positioning approximately 20% below alternatives.
- **B2B channel development:** segment distributors, retailers and pet-care partners; create prospecting, onboarding and reorder follow-up routines.
- **Campaign execution:** plan promotional activity with defined audience, offer, budget, sales owner and post-campaign review.
- **Retention:** track first order, reorder interval, dormant accounts and customer feedback.

## What a manager should measure next
Lead-to-first-order conversion, active distributors, revenue by channel, reorder rate, average order value, gross margin by SKU, campaign incremental lift, stock availability and returns.

## Evidence to attach
Redacted campaign brief, approved sales summary, budget approval, invoice/order summary, distributor onboarding tracker and campaign-period comparison. Do not upload confidential customer or employer data.

## Learning
A strong growth case must connect positioning and channel activity to revenue while distinguishing revenue, gross profit, contribution and marketing return.
