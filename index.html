How I approach a business problem

01

Diagnose

Understand business model, customer, market, funnel, baseline data and constraints.

02

Prioritise

Identify the highest-impact bottleneck and define a measurable objective.

03

Execute

Build channel plan, budget, ownership, timeline, creative and sales handoff.

04

Measure

Track leading and lagging indicators, validate attribution and compare against baseline.

05

Optimise

Run controlled tests, stop low-return activity and scale validated opportunities.

06

Communicate

Present results, risks, learnings and next decisions in a clear management report.
